# creep privacy policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Creep ("the Bot"), a Discord bot, may process information when users interact with its commands and features.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Creep may process the following information:

* **Discord User IDs:** To identify users interacting with the Bot.
* **Discord Server IDs:** To identify the server where a command is used.
* **Command Inputs:** Information submitted when using the Bot's commands.
* **Moderation Data:** Information related to moderation actions, such as warnings or timeouts, if these features are enabled.
* **Server Configuration:** Settings required for the Bot's functionality, if applicable.

The Bot is not intended to request Discord passwords or payment card information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

* Provide the Bot's commands and features.
* Execute moderation actions requested by authorized users.
* Maintain the Bot's functionality and troubleshoot errors.
* Prevent abuse of the Bot.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Some features may require information to be stored locally or through a database.

The type of information stored and how long it is retained depend on the Bot's implementation and configuration.

Information should be deleted when it is no longer necessary for the relevant feature, subject to applicable requirements.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Creep does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord or other service providers involved in operating the Bot, where necessary for the service to function.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Creep operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's applicable policies.

The Bot's developer does not control Discord's independent data-processing practices.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the Bot. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should not submit sensitive personal information through the Bot's commands.

## 8. Your Requests

For questions about information handled by Creep, or requests concerning stored information, contact the Bot's developer through the official support channel, if available.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Creep is not intended to collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide its features. Users should follow Discord's applicable age requirements.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy link.

## 11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the Bot's developer through the official support channel, if available.
